## About the Business application

We now have new APIs for **the Business application**:

- Create or edit a product
- Get my product catalog
- Get product catalog from another business number
- Get product data by ID
- Delete a product by ID
- Manage labels and collections
- Configure company data

<Warning>
All new APIs **only work** on the Business application, and are also **only available** for the Business application using **"Multi Device"**.
</Warning>
